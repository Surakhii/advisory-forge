# GHSA submission checklist

## Identifiers and scope

- [ ] Advisory title is specific and actionable.
- [ ] Affected package/repository and version ranges are exact.
- [ ] CWE is selected and reflects the root cause.
- [ ] Credits and reporting relationship are accurate.

## Technical review

- [ ] Reproduction steps work from a clean environment.
- [ ] Proof of concept is harmless and contains no live secrets.
- [ ] Root cause and security boundary are clearly explained.
- [ ] Impact is separated into confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
- [ ] CVSS vector and score are included together.

## Remediation and references

- [ ] Fixed version or commit is identified.
- [ ] Workarounds and their limits are documented.
- [ ] Related advisories, issues, or pull requests are linked.

## Publication

- [ ] Vendor has been notified through the security channel.
- [ ] Embargo date and timezone are confirmed.
- [ ] Credit and recognition preferences are confirmed.
- [ ] Public text contains no credential, token, or private endpoint.
